DongFangHong Satellite Platforms Pack 0.3.4

The pinned package has four parts and 284 native material slots. The five UV masks select photovoltaic cells on the DFH-2 drum and the DFH-3, DFH-4 and DFH-5 wings. Chang'e uses its own atlas mask. The masks follow the blue cell color in the authored textures with Gaussian smoothing at the cell edges. The finish uses shininess 0.95 and specular color #e7e7e7.

DFH-2 binds the mask to both meshes of the drum. The antenna and foil retain their source materials. The gallery shows DFH-2's fixed cells and the deployed DFH-3, DFH-4 and DFH-5 wings in the hangar and on the runway.

The runtime check exercised both DFH-2 variants, the DFH-3, Chang'e and Beidou variants, and both supplied variants of DFH-4 and DFH-5. It checked active masked geometry and the specular constants in both scenes.
