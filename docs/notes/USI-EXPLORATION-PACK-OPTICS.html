USI Exploration Pack v112.0.1

The native inventory covered 74 parts. Five parts received optical profiles and were photographed individually in the SPH and on the runway at a solar elevation of 82.94°–82.96°.

Part ShineFix coverage
AES Pod Transparent and reflective hatch glass, plus reflections on both exterior lamp lenses.

Otter high-powered spotlight Reflection on the circular lens with the original lamp output.
DERP Concentrated Photovoltaic Array Reflection on the photovoltaic cells of all six deployed leaves.

The three cabins use 80% opacity, 0.95 shininess and #e7e7e7 specular color. Their reflection and transmission use the same glass mask. Native sightline probes and paired captures with and without the visible IVA confirmed interior visibility in both scenes. Each cabin had one crew member on its assigned seat. The editor crew preview matched the assignment, and the flight check verified the seated IVA kerbal. The HERP and Otter window lights and the AES and Otter exterior lights passed both switch states. Both DERP captures show the active faces of all six leaves, and KSP confirmed full deployment and all six renderer bindings.

The DERP rescue pod canopy is a parachute. The PackRat external seat has no optical surface and was outside this gallery.
