Halloween 0.9.9.9-prerelease optical review

KSP loaded 20 parts and 49 exterior slots. ShineFix matches the two eye meshes on all four Kraken props, the glass helmets on the blue and pink props, the Kerba Lamp jar and the Koffin pane. The selected slots use 0.95 and #e7e7e7. The white Kraken's additive effect and the candle flames retain their authored shaders.

The first same-camera comparison showed that Unity's premultiplied Standard shader obscured the contents behind the clear helmets, jar and pane. The final profiles use ShineFix's authored texture opacity path for these four sheets. The final comparison shows the source shapes behind the reflections in both scenes. The Kerba Lamp completed its native on/off cycle on the runway. None of these props has an IVA seat or a photovoltaic array.
