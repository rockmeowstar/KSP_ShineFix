Kerbal Aircraft Expansion 4:2.8.1.2

C2-B Horizon Cockpit. Selected five blue window islands in the source diffuse atlas and mapped the mirrored UVs to nine visible panes. The reflection and transparency profiles use the same smoothed mask, 0.95, #e7e7e7 and 0.80 opacity. The model has no native window emission texture or switch.

KSP verified the supplied GenericSpace3 IVA through all nine panes, individually in the hangar and on the runway. The mean IVA A/B delta ranged from 12.17 to 31.37 in the hangar and 5.84 to 14.48 on the runway. The one-part craft assigned two crew members to native seats 0 and 2. The gallery shows both scenes from above the windscreen.
