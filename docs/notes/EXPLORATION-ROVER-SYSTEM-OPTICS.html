Exploration Rover System by ASET 0.4.0.7

The native inventory covers eleven parts and 625 material slots. The other crew modules use opaque exterior atlases and clear decal plates. Cabin glazing has its own ExtGlassObj mesh and tint texture. Its reflection and transmission share the same mask at 80% opacity, 0.95 shininess and #e7e7e7 specular color. The cabin has no authored window emission texture. The native interior and assigned seats were checked in both scenes.

The spotlight reflection is limited to the lit regions of the LEDs mesh. Its extension animation reached the end pose in both scenes and the actual light switched on and off in flight. The cabin navigation and front and back wheel indicator profiles use the source emission islands for the reflective mask. The wheel camera positions were selected by ray tests against the actual geometry, with five unoccluded lens samples per wheel in each scene. The authored navigation and spotlight emission maps remain in use.
