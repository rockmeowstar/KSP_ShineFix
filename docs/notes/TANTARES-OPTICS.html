Tantares v28.0 optical review

The native material inventory covers 326 parts and 1,292 material slots. There are 60 crewed parts and 55 configured IVA names. Window-like renderer names identify 48 crewed candidates. The other 12 crewed parts also require a model and UV check. Three uncrewed candidates with window-like names belong to Vostok instrumentation or telescopes.

The 21 parts with ModuleDeployableSolarPanel use eight authored specular atlases. The masks select the cell islands from the specular maps, then apply the shared Gaussian filter. Each profile binds to the moving cell meshes and their exact atlas at shininess 0.95 and specular color #e7e7e7. These profiles have no transparency rule. Rebuilding the eight DDS files and profiles produced identical hashes. Static checks matched all 21 profiles to the native renderer and atlas inventory. All 21 arrays were captured deployed and facing the camera in KSP. The Aquila Type B craft loaded its selected B9 subtype in both scenes and bound its three extension leaves. The gallery shows Type B.

UV projections confirmed dedicated glass meshes for 48 crewed parts and three uncrewed parts. The cabin profiles use the same full pane mesh for reflection and transparency, with 0.80 opacity and 50% native window emission. The uncrewed Vostok guidance core and two telescopes have reflection-only profiles. The other 12 crewed parts have 21 exterior hull meshes. Where their atlases are shared with confirmed glazing, the hull UVs do not overlap the interior of those glass islands.

The pinned package has no usable Libra IVA asset and gives no IVA name to the four crewed MEK window candidates. The native harness checks these parts for retained reflections and an opaque hull. The first native gallery used 72 single-part crafts. It measured a zero image difference with and without projected IVA for both Acamar parts. Ray probes found an opaque backing at 17–28 mm behind those exterior panes.

The backing generator covers 37 shell meshes across 31 parts. It requires a matching model transform and ray evidence of an obstruction inside the aperture. It trims each shell within the measured pane perimeter and depth. KSP confirmed all three Acamar panes in both scenes. Mean image differences with the IVA shown ranged from 20.52 to 37.21 in the hangar and 6.43 to 24.98 on the runway. The native toggle restored each visual shell while preserving its collider.

Native retakes confirmed the deeper Eridani, Grus, Tucana and Soyuz hull cuts, along with Nashira's two panes. The conical Soyuz orbital module had a second skin up to 229 mm behind the recessed side port. Andromeda had the opposite problem. Its side windows had solid backing less than 3 mm behind the glass. Lowering the cut threshold for those two capsules opened all three panes on each. The inner translucent iw1 pane stays in native IVA and is hidden in the projected exterior IVA. The final Andromeda run measured visible IVA in all six windows in both scenes. Hull toggles passed.

The gallery contains 72 complete pairs. Each pair comes from one native run and uses the same single-part craft in the hangar and on the runway at 83° solar elevation. The source manifest records the selected run for every pair.
