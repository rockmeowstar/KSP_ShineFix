The optics gallery covers all 13 affected standalone parts in the SPH and on the runway with KSP Community Fixes 1.41.1.

The seven cabs bind the same mask for reflection and transparency on nine material slots. The profiles use 80% glass opacity, 20% transmission, shininess 0.95, specular color #e7e7e7 and 50% of authored window emission. Native screenshots with and without the exterior IVA verify that the cabin contributes through the glazing. Mirror and lamp masks are scoped to the corresponding materials. Switchable lamp geometry uses the mod's authored Glow state.

The Club Car has six exterior seats and Gator utility has two. The test occupies positions 1, 3 and 5 in the Club Car and position 1 in the Gator, verifies the same occupants and empty seats in both scenes, and checks the first occupant's repair kit after launch. The editor uses the actual seat pivot and stock seated EVA pose. The launch fix respects stock manifest seat indices on multi-seat exterior parts when those indices are valid.
