Knes v1.9.9 optical review

KSP loaded 213 parts, 958 material slots and 19 crewed parts. Nine parts have a photovoltaic module. The working integration is under GameData/000_ShineFixDynamic/085_Knes.

The nine photovoltaic parts use three shared atlases. The cell masks select the connected blue islands in the ATV, Asterix and MRK diffuse textures. The ATV and MRK profiles also restrict the effect to the panel leaf renderers. The NanoSat and its fixed panel share the Asterix atlas with their housings. Static validation matched all nine parts to the native material inventory.

UV checks confirmed window atlas use on French Hermes, European Hermes, the two crewed MRK capsules, Crew Pod 0.9375 and the three station modules. The station hull mask covers the glass island shared by the Crystal Factory, Hab and Science Lab. The adapter and endcap meshes that use the same habitat atlas have no UV overlap with that island. The two European Hermes bodies share one mask, derived from four islands in each paint variant. Both optical effects use the same mask per part. The crewed profiles specify 0.80 opacity and use the native window-emission control at half intensity.

The cargo MRK uses two of the painted window islands and gets reflection on those areas. The Vesta cat pod's named porthole gets an exact-renderer reflection. The non-emissive StarRider and StarWatcher portholes use blue texels on their exact cap meshes. KSP confirmed both interiors through those ports in the hangar and on the runway.

Three hatch masks cover 35 parts with sampled UV overlap on the exact hatch renderers. The KSP run confirmed the reflection binding on all 50 integration targets. Transmission checks use the KSP-projected interiors on the 14 crewed parts that have IVA models and a hatch mask. The runtime probe records the first intersections with the hull or interior.

The French Hermes crew module and docking module had opaque hull surfaces behind two and three hatch openings, respectively. The European Hermes and HRM had a backing 24–31 mm behind the recessed hatches. The cuts follow the measured apertures and leave the colliders in place. KSP verified visible interiors and the shell toggle for all four parts in both scenes. The French Hermes rear hatch is inside the cargo bay. KSP verified that port from along the open bay axis, then restored the doors for the main gallery frame. Mean local pixel differences with the projected interior enabled were 36.69 in the hangar and 30.53 on the runway.

Both fixed NanoSat arrays now show the working face in the hangar and on the runway. The KSP editor camera has a 0.5 m near clip distance on these runs. Moving it to 1.25 m and reducing the field of view kept the cells in frame. The native reflection-off comparisons and the screenshots confirm the effect independently of the gold frames.

The gallery contains 50 complete pairs. The runway shots use the survey's 83° solar elevation target within its native tolerance. Both scenes of each pair come from the run listed in the capture-source file.
