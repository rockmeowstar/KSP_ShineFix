The seven window profiles share coverage between reflection and transparency. They use 0.95 shininess, #e7e7e7 specular color, 80% opacity and 50% of the authored window emission. IVA models use their native placements and seat transforms.

The authored IVA window matches the main canopy longitudinally. The forward pane extends about 0.34 m farther toward the nose. The mesh patches follow the forward aperture and alter 34 Viper and 30 Kestrel backing triangles. Kestrel retains both submeshes. The fixed-camera test probes through both layers of glazing along the sightline toward the instrument bay.

KSP verified the seven occupied cabins, their window light switches, the full solar deployment and exposed cells, and the open docking lenses. Viper, Serpent and Kestrel passed the canopy checks in both scenes. The Viper and Kestrel shell checks confirmed restoration with transparency off and reopening with transparency on. Nine single-part image pairs are in the gallery. The runway captures measured 82.901–82.959° solar elevation. The manifest identifies the front-facing Tuna and Raven retakes.
